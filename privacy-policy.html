Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Crochet Toolkit ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App").

2. Data Collection and Usage

2.1 Local Data Storage

The primary function of the App is to help you manage your crochet and knitting projects. All personal data related to your projects, inventory, counters, work sessions, and quotes is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to any external server, cloud storage, or third party, except when you explicitly choose to export or share a backup file or PDF document.

2.2 Advertising Data (AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of the App, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a mobile advertising service provided by Google LLC.

AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information from your device to provide personalized ads and analyze ad performance. This data may include:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • Usage data (e.g., ad interactions, impressions)
  • Approximate location data (if available and permitted)

For more detailed information on how Google AdMob collects and uses data, please refer to Google's Privacy & Terms.

2.3 In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Our App may offer in-app purchases or subscriptions (e.g., an "Ads-Free" version). All such transactions are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card information, billing address, or any other sensitive financial data. The information regarding your purchase history and subscription status is securely managed by Google. For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your locally stored personal data with any third parties. Data collected by AdMob is shared with Google and its advertising partners as described in their respective privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 12 in jurisdictions such as Brazil under the LGPD). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child, we will take prompt steps to delete such information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, US States, Brazil LGPD, Australia & New Zealand)

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws—such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe, US State Privacy Laws (including CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Law No. 13.709/2018), Australia's Privacy Act 1988 (including the Australian Privacy Principles), or New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 (Information Privacy Principles)—you may have specific rights regarding your personal data:

Local App Data: Because our App stores all your project, counter, inventory, and cost calculation data locally on your device, you maintain complete and direct control to access, modify, export, or delete this data directly within the App at any time without needing to contact us.

Advertising & Third-Party Services: Data collected by Google AdMob is processed by Google LLC and its advertising partners as described in their respective privacy policies. Depending on your jurisdiction:

  • US State Privacy Laws & GDPR: This data sharing may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under certain US State Privacy Laws, or require consent under the GDPR. You have the right to opt-out or withdraw your consent at any time via the "Privacy & Consents" section in the App's settings menu (where available) or via your device settings.
  • Brazil (LGPD): Under Article 18 of the LGPD, users residing in Brazil have the right to obtain confirmation of data processing, access, correction, anonymization, deletion, information about third-party sharing, and revocation of consent. You may exercise these rights or address any data protection inquiries by contacting us at the email address provided in Section 7.
  • Australia & New Zealand: In accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 (IPPs), you have the right to request access to and correction of personal information held about you. Any transmission of device identifiers by third-party advertising services outside Australia or New Zealand (e.g., to Google's global servers) is conducted pursuant to Google's established global data protection and security standards.

Additionally, you can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your advertising identifier at any time at the device level using your operating system settings (e.g., on Android via Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID, or on iOS via Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
